The plan

  1. Step 1 of 5

    1 🌍 Ways to give

    • A “How to give” page on fercthailand.org, one box per country.
    • Help with leaving Myriad.
    • From the USA, with a tax receipt: CAF America.
    • Thai tax receipts: e-Donation. We check with the treasurer.
    We do
    Compare the ways to give, with costs and time. Prepare the papers for leaving Myriad and for CAF America. Write a short email to US and Australian donors. Build the “How to give” page.
    You do
    Tell the treasurer we will ask about e-Donation. Give us an administrator login for fercthailand.org. Sign the applications. Send the email to donors.
    When
    November, before the 28th
    Cost to FERC
    Our work: free. CAF America keeps a fee from each US gift. We ask them how much.
    More about step 1

    FERC is leaving Myriad. (Myriad USA and Myriad Australia pass US and Australian gifts on to FERC today.) We help with that paperwork. Then we add a “How to give” page to fercthailand.org: one box per country, each saying whether the donor gets a tax receipt.

    ThailandBank transfer, as now. Since 1 January 2026, a Thai tax deduction for a gift to a foundation goes through e-Donation, the Revenue Department’s online system. We ask the treasurer whether FERC is registered. If not, we find out how FERC can register.
    SwitzerlandBank transfer with a Swiss tax receipt, as now. The website explains it more clearly.
    USACAF America, a US charity, takes the gift, gives the donor a US tax receipt, and sends the money to FERC. FERC’s page on Every.org, a US giving website, works through Myriad USA, so it may close when FERC leaves Myriad.
    AustraliaAn Australian charity that can give Australian tax receipts, such as Global Development Group, takes gifts for FERC. This is not ready in November. Until then: bank transfer to FERC’s Thai account.
    UK, Netherlands, othersBank transfer to FERC’s Thai account, for example with Wise, a money-transfer service. No tax receipt in the donor’s country.

    The sample giving page on hongdam.net shows the idea. The “How to give” page goes on fercthailand.org.

  2. Step 2 of 5

    2 ✉️ Newsletter

    • Move the list from MailPoet (free up to 500) to Brevo (free for more).
    • Brevo sends 300 emails a day, so 500 people take two days.
    • The first newsletter tells donors the new ways to give.
    We do
    Move the list to Brevo. People who unsubscribed stay unsubscribed. Make a FERC newsletter design and a sign-up form on fercthailand.org. Write a one-page guide. The first newsletter tells donors about the new ways to give from step 1.
    You do
    Say yes to the move. Then you send each newsletter. The guide shows how.
    When
    November
    Cost to FERC
    Free
    More about step 2

    MailPoet, your newsletter tool, is free up to 500 contacts, and FERC has reached 500. Brevo, another newsletter service, is free for more than 500 contacts. It sends 300 emails a day. So a newsletter to 500 people goes out over two days: 300 on the first day, the other 200 the next day with one more click. Free Brevo newsletters end with a small line, “Sent with Brevo”. (MailerLite, a similar service, is now free only up to 250 contacts.)

🤝 Pay in trade

FERC can pay up to half of any extra in trade instead of money. For example:

  • A line at the bottom of fercthailand.org, “Website and giving page: Hongdam”, one thank-you in the newsletter, and permission to show FERC’s pages as our work.
  • Our name on the thank-you slide and a table card at the fundraiser on 29 November.
  • Our job posts sent to FERC alumni who said yes to job news. Adults only. FERC sends them, not us.

Introductions are separate. They earn FERC money (next section).

🏘️ For all of Chiang Mai

Many Chiang Mai charities and clubs have donors abroad, as FERC does.

Other groups can get a giving page like FERC’s. Gifts go to the group’s own bank account, with a record of each gift. Price for other groups: ฿30,000 to set up, then ฿10,000 a month, stop any month. For FERC, its giving page is part of step 1 and free.

If you introduce someone who then hires us, for this or any other work, we give FERC 20% of what they pay us in their first year, as their payments reach us.
