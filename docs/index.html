The plan

  1. 1

    Every donor can give, in their own country

    We take the Myriad exit off your desk. fercthailand.org gets one clear “How to give” box per country, with a note on tax receipts.

    ThailandBank transfer, as today. A Thai tax deduction for a gift to a foundation now goes through the Revenue Department’s e-Donation system; we check with the treasurer whether FERC is registered, and help register if not.
    SwitzerlandBank transfer with a Swiss tax receipt, as today. Clearer on the website.
    USACAF America, a US charity, passes gifts on to FERC; the donor gets a US tax receipt. FERC’s Every.org page runs through Myriad USA, so it may stop when FERC leaves Myriad.
    AustraliaA partner that already has Australian tax status, such as Global Development Group. This takes longer than November; until then, direct transfer.
    UK, Netherlands, othersDirect transfer to FERC’s Thai account, for example with Wise. No tax receipt in the donor’s country.
    We do
    Compare the routes with their costs and set-up time. Prepare the papers for leaving Myriad and for the new routes. Write a short email to US and Australian donors. Build the “How to give” page.
    You do
    The treasurer’s answer on e-Donation. Your signature on the applications.
    When
    November, before the 28th
    Cost to FERC
    CAF America takes a fee from each gift; we ask them for a quote.
  2. 2

    A newsletter list with room to grow

    MailPoet’s free plan stops at 500 contacts. Brevo’s free plan has no contact limit and sends 300 emails a day. A 500-person newsletter goes to the first 300 on day one; the next day, one click in Brevo sends the rest. The free plan shows a small Brevo logo.

    We do
    Move the list to Brevo, unsubscribes included. Make a FERC newsletter template and a signup form on fercthailand.org. Write a one-page guide.
    You do
    Approve, then send newsletters yourself.
    When
    November
    Cost to FERC
    Free

  4. 4

    More people see FERC

    • Two posts a week on Facebook and LinkedIn, November and December, around the talk, the fundraiser and Christmas

    Free
  5. 5

    From January: sponsor reports in less time

    Today Heinz collects about 100 student reports twice a year, you turn them into sponsor reports, and the master list and the treasurer’s list are kept apart.

    • One shared list in place of two, with a column for each student’s consent
    • A student’s report typed in once; the sponsor’s report comes out ready to send
    • One view of which reports are in, which are missing, which are sent

    It holds details of minors, so it is private to the FERC team, and works through CMSEO and the schools as now.

    When
    From January, if the board wants it
