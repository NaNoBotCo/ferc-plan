The plan

  1. 1

    1. Donations from each country

    FERC is leaving Myriad. (Myriad USA and Myriad Australia pass US and Australian gifts on to FERC today.) We help with that paperwork. Then we add a “How to give” page to fercthailand.org: one box per country, each saying whether the donor gets a tax receipt.

    ThailandBank transfer, as now. Since 1 January 2026, a Thai tax deduction for a gift to a foundation goes through e-Donation, the Revenue Department’s online system. We ask the treasurer whether FERC is registered. If not, we find out what registering needs.
    SwitzerlandBank transfer with a Swiss tax receipt, as now. The website explains it more clearly.
    USACAF America, a US charity, takes the gift, gives the donor a US tax receipt, and sends the money to FERC. FERC’s page on Every.org, a US giving website, works through Myriad USA, so it may close when FERC leaves Myriad.
    AustraliaAn Australian charity that can give Australian tax receipts, such as Global Development Group, takes gifts for FERC. This is not ready in November. Until then: bank transfer to FERC’s Thai account.
    UK, Netherlands, othersBank transfer to FERC’s Thai account, for example with Wise, a money-transfer service. No tax receipt in the donor’s country.
    We do
    Compare the ways to give, with costs and time. Prepare the papers for leaving Myriad and for CAF America. Write a short email to US and Australian donors. Build the “How to give” page.
    You do
    Ask the treasurer about e-Donation. Sign the applications. Send the email to donors.
    When
    November, before the 28th
    Cost to FERC
    Our work: free. CAF America keeps a fee from each US gift. We ask them how much.
  2. 2

    2. A newsletter for more than 500 people

    MailPoet, your newsletter tool, is free up to 500 contacts, and FERC has reached 500. Brevo, another newsletter service, is free for more than 500 contacts. It sends 300 emails a day. So a newsletter to 500 people goes out over two days: 300 on the first day, the other 200 the next day with one more click. Free Brevo newsletters end with a small line, “Sent with Brevo”.

    We do
    Move the list to Brevo. People who unsubscribed stay unsubscribed. Make a FERC newsletter design and a sign-up form on fercthailand.org. Write a one-page guide.
    You do
    Say yes to the move. Then send each newsletter yourself, with the guide.
    When
    November
    Cost to FERC
    Free

For all of Chiang Mai

Many Chiang Mai charities and clubs have the same problem as FERC: donors abroad, no easy way to give, no clear record of gifts.

Any group can get the same giving page as FERC, with a record of every gift, paid into its own bank account. Price for other groups: ฿30,000 to set up, then ฿10,000 a month, stop any month.

If you introduce someone who then hires us, for this or any other work, we give FERC 20% of what they pay us in their first year, as their payments reach us.
